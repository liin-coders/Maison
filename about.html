05 The Experience

The 5-Sensory Gastronomic Ritual

Dining at MAISON is curated as a multi-act symphony that engages all five human senses in harmony.

Multi-Sensorial

01. Taste (Gustatory)

Harmonious balance of umami, subtle acidity, and natural sweetness extracted through slow reduction.

02. Aroma (Olfactory)

03. Vision (Visual Plating)
